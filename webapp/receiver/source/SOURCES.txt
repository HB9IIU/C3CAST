"source/" is the folder next to this file. The receivers are separate programs under their own licences;
C3CAST itself and the ESP32-DATV firmware are under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (see LICENSE in the package folder).

Microsoft's .NET support libraries in tuner-service/bin (System.Buffers, System.Memory, System.Numerics.Vectors, System.Runtime.CompilerServices.Unsafe, System.Diagnostics.DiagnosticSource):
    MIT licence, part of .NET; pulled in by the packages above. No source obligation.

The same libusb, LibUsbDotNet, FTDI wrapper, Json.NET and Serilog files are in the H266-Player folder; their sources and licence
texts are in H266-Player/source and H266-Player/licenses.

The receive-side ffmpeg is the one in tools/ffmpeg (the same file that transmits): see tools/ffmpeg/README.txt and tools/ffmpeg/source.

Origin of datvrx.exe, rtl_iq.exe and the three native libraries: they are byte-for-byte the files of DATVision 1.0.0
(https://github.com/HB9IIU/DATVision, tag v1.0.0), whose source is the zip named above. The web address is only for
information; the source you are entitled to is in this folder.
