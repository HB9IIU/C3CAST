## Recommendations for review

- Keep preset `faster`; `fast` missed real time even at 640x360/30 fps.
- 125 kS/s: current 640x360/30 fps is a good candidate on this PC (2.12–2.28x across the two FECs).
- 250 kS/s: 960x540/15 fps is a candidate (1.45x at FEC 3/4); existing 30 fps is marginal.
- 333 kS/s: 1280x720/15 fps worked live but has limited offline margin (1.23x). 640x360/15 fps is a more conservative candidate (1.79x).
- 500 kS/s: 640x360/15 fps is a conservative candidate (1.47x). 960x540/15 fps was only 1.01x; 1600x900 is too slow.
- 1000 kS/s: even 640x360/15 fps is marginal on the recorded sample (1.05x). Its short live test passed, but that does not establish reliable performance with motion. Prefer existing H.265/NVENC at this rate pending further VVC work.
- Retain the existing 80% VVC target budget initially. At 30 fps, the baseline sample ran roughly 11–17% above video target; at 15 fps the average video rate was closer to target or below it. Short TS file averages can exceed configured muxrate slightly because of timing/startup/flush behavior; all measured averages were below calculated channel capacity. This does not validate every instantaneous burst.
- Do not change H.265 profiles. Review video quality before accepting smaller VVC pictures or lower frame rates. No subjective quality ranking or SSIM/VMAF claim is made by this timing benchmark.

## Limits and remaining tests

Only the original 30 fps baseline was tested at both FECs. Alternative profiles and live checks used FEC 3/4. These are short trials, not long-duration or on-air validation. PCR timing, receiver lip-sync, RF quality and transmitter queue behavior were not measured here. Live startup is time to the first TS bytes on the PC, not first picture at a receiver.

Initial recorded-source runs used the generic lowpass audio filter and reported clipping warnings. Later conservative low-resolution and live tests used the application microphone compressor/limiter. This work does not retune audio.

Full local evidence: `captures/vvc-camera-benchmark/environment.json`, `results/results.json`, per-test commands, logs and TS samples. The original camera recording and all sample content stay in ignored local captures; they are not added to the repository. A partially interrupted larger `fast` trial may have a file/log but is excluded from the completed results.

## Repeating the baseline

```powershell
.venv-host/Scripts/python.exe tools/benchmark_vvc.py --input captures/vvc-camera-benchmark/camera-original.mkv --output captures/vvc-camera-benchmark/retest
```

Use a fresh output directory. Run tests sequentially with other CPU-heavy work stopped. The script performs baseline tests, stops slower-preset exploration if the smallest trial misses the margin, and tries 15 fps where the baseline is marginal. Additional size/thread/live experiments were invoked through its `run()` helper. The script does not modify application settings or open an RF device.
